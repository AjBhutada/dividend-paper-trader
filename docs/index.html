    Rules the bot follows
    
    
      
  • NSE EQ shares with a market cap of ₹500–2,500 cr, a dividend of at least 0.5% of the price, at least ₹20 lakh traded a day, price at least ₹10, no circuit-locked days, no bonus, split or rights issue nearby.
    • 
      
  • Buy day: the later of 10 trading days before the ex-date and the first trading day after the record-date news (it must be at least 2 days before the ex-date). The plan is made at 8:27 am and checked again at 9:05 am with the newest announcements.
    • 
      
  • Buying: 5 slices (9:30, 10:30, 11:30 am, 12:45 and 2:00 pm), each only in a calm minute (no volume spike), filled against the live offers on the Upstox feed, never more than 10% of the volume traded so far.
    • 
      
  • Selling: half at 3:05 pm and the rest at 3:20 pm on the last cum day, against the live bids. A 10% stop-loss below the buy price is watched on every tick from the day after buying.
    • 
      
  • Size: 10% of the account per share as margin, reset at the start of each quarter (₹50,000 now); twice that for cash trades with a dividend of 1.5% or more. MTF only on stocks in Kite's, Upstox's or Groww's real lists (in that order), otherwise cash.
    • 
      
  • Costs: per-order brokerage, STT, stamp duty, exchange and SEBI fees, GST, DP and pledge charges, and MTF interest. Backup trades on 1-minute candles also carry a price-impact estimate.
    • 
      
  • The 9 pm run marks positions at NSE's official close, and trades anything the live trader missed on 1-minute candles. Any problem opens a GitHub issue, which emails the owner.
